# Jagt (Hunt)

**Drinking factor:** 3–10
**Players:** 10+
**Time:** 10+ min
**Equipment:** 1 glass per participant, 1 dice cup, 2 dice per 2 participants

## Introduction

In Jagt, the hunter can just as quickly become the hunted. Pace is what drives the game — more speed and energy makes it wilder. How much you drink along the way is up to you, but in practice it tends to climb right along with the tempo.

## Rules

1. Before the game starts, everyone fills a glass with a self-chosen amount of beer — there's no fixed rule for how much.
2. Only every other player starts with a dice cup holding two dice.
3. Players with a cup take turns rolling, passing the cup to the left after each roll. Rolling a pair lets you break the rhythm and roll again immediately instead.
4. If the cup gets passed to a player who already has one, that player is skipped this round — and has to chug and refill their glass before they're allowed to roll again.
5. If you drop a die on the floor, you have to chug and refill your glass before continuing.
6. If you have to leave the game temporarily, your neighbor keeps track of how many times you get skipped while you're away — and you have to chug that many times before you're allowed to roll again once you're back.
